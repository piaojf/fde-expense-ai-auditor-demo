# Distributed assets

Only synthetic invoice/policy test fixtures are included. Their source generators are `scripts/generate_invoice_fixtures.py`, `scripts/generate_policy_fixtures.py`, `scripts/generate_publication_fixture.py`, and `scripts/generate_public_policy.py`. They describe fictional companies and identify themselves as test/demo materials with no financial or tax authority.

The original private hotel policy, its later amendment, historical provider responses, screenshots of internal runtime state, ledgers, database generations, credentials, OCR traineddata, fonts and downloaded dependencies are excluded.

No project-wide open-source license is granted in this snapshot. Dependency packages retain their own licenses; install them using their package managers rather than copying their environments into this repository.
