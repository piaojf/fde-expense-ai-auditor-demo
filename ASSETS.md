# Distributed assets

Synthetic invoice/policy test fixtures are included. Their source generators are `scripts/generate_invoice_fixtures.py`, `scripts/generate_policy_fixtures.py`, `scripts/generate_publication_fixture.py`, and `scripts/generate_public_policy.py`. They describe fictional companies and identify themselves as test/demo materials with no financial or tax authority.

The five screenshots in `docs/images/` were supplied by the project owner to illustrate the local enterprise demonstration. The owner confirmed the invoice number and hotel name visible in the submission screenshot are synthetic. They document the interface and do not establish that the full enterprise workflow is reproducible from the public quick start.

The original private hotel policy, its later amendment, raw historical provider response files, other internal runtime screenshots, ledgers, database generations, credentials, OCR traineddata, fonts and downloaded dependencies are excluded.

No project-wide open-source license is granted in this snapshot. Dependency packages retain their own licenses; install them using their package managers rather than copying their environments into this repository.
